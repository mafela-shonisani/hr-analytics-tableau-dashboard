# HR Analytics Dashboard | Tableau

## 1. Business Problem

Human Resource departments collect large amounts of employee information, but analysing this information to understand workforce trends can be challenging.

Management needs to understand the organisation's workforce composition, employee demographics, departmental distribution, salary patterns, performance ratings, and employee retention.

The main business problem addressed in this project is:

**How can employee data be analysed and visualised to identify workforce patterns and provide insights that support better HR decision-making?**

## 2. Project Objectives

- Analyse employee demographics and workforce composition.
- Identify departments with the highest employee numbers.
- Examine employee distribution by gender and education level.
- Analyse employee performance ratings.
- Investigate hiring and termination trends over time.
- Explore salary differences across departments.
- Examine employee distribution across geographical locations.

## 3. Dataset Overview

The dataset contains 8,950 employee records and 14 columns.

The information includes:
- Employee ID and personal details.
- Gender and date of birth.
- Education level.
- Department and job title.
- Salary.
- Hiring and termination dates.
- Performance ratings.
- State and city.

## 4. Dashboard Interpretation and Key Findings

The dashboard consists of two main sections: HR Summary and HR Details.

### A. HR Summary

This section provides an overview of the organisation's workforce.

**Workforce Status**

Out of 8,950 employee records:
- 7,984 employees (89.2%) had no recorded termination date.
- 966 employees (10.8%) had recorded termination dates.

This indicates that most employee records represent employees without a recorded termination date. The termination figures provide an opportunity for HR to investigate employee departures and retention patterns.

**Departmental Analysis**

The Operations department had the largest workforce, with 2,718 employees.

Other departments included:
- Sales: 1,835 employees.
- Customer Service: 1,673 employees.
- IT: 1,382 employees.
- Marketing: 718 employees.
- Finance: 452 employees.
- HR: 172 employees.

The distribution shows that employee numbers are concentrated in Operations, Sales and Customer Service.

**Geographical Analysis**

New York accounted for 6,270 employee records, making it the largest employee location in the dataset.

The dashboard also distinguishes between the headquarters and branch locations, allowing users to explore geographical workforce distribution.

**Employee Demographics**

The gender distribution was:
- Male: 4,801 (53.6%).
- Female: 4,149 (46.4%).

In terms of education:
- Bachelor's degree: 5,416 employees.
- High School: 1,819 employees.
- Master's degree: 1,237 employees.
- PhD: 478 employees.

Bachelor's degree holders represented the largest education group.

**Performance Analysis**

Employee performance ratings were distributed as follows:

- Good: 3,763 employees (42.0%).
- Satisfactory: 2,498 employees (27.9%).
- Excellent: 1,566 employees (17.5%).
- Needs Improvement: 1,123 employees (12.5%).

The Good category accounted for the largest share of performance ratings.

However, the Needs Improvement category also represents an area where HR could investigate potential training, development and performance-support opportunities.

**Hiring and Termination Trends**

The hiring analysis showed that 2017 recorded the highest number of employee hires, with 1,560 records.

For employee terminations, 2023 recorded the highest number, with 174 records.

These trends can help HR understand changes in workforce movement over time and identify periods that may require further investigation.

### B. HR Details

The HR Details dashboard provides a more detailed view of individual employee information.

It allows users to explore employee records using filters and interactive charts.

The dashboard focuses on:

- Employee information and identification.
- Employee age and demographic distribution.
- Department and job title distribution.
- Geographical location.
- Salary information.
- Employment status.
- Length of employment.

This section makes it possible to move beyond overall workforce statistics and investigate specific employee groups.

### C. Salary Analysis

The average recorded salary across the dataset was approximately 70,964.

The departmental salary analysis showed:

| Department | Average Salary |
|---|---:|
| IT | 81,926 |
| Finance | 76,451 |
| Sales | 76,205 |
| Marketing | 67,659 |
| Customer Service | 65,838 |
| Operations | 65,400 |
| HR | 64,145 |

IT recorded the highest average salary, while HR recorded the lowest.

This comparison highlights differences in salary distribution across departments. It does not, by itself, establish the reasons behind those differences.

## 5. Business Insights

Based on the analysis, several areas deserve management attention:

1. **Workforce concentration:** Operations, Sales and Customer Service account for a large proportion of the workforce.
2. **Employee retention:** The 966 records with termination dates provide an opportunity to investigate employee departures.
3. **Performance management:** Although Good was the most common rating, 1,123 employees received Needs Improvement.
4. **Salary differences:** Average salaries varied across departments, with IT recording the highest average.
5. **Workforce movement:** Hiring and termination numbers changed across the years, suggesting the need to monitor workforce trends.
